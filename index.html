Play mode

Automatic: tapping a cheese drives there, stabs, and brings it home. Guest aims: tapping a cheese drives there and shows ◀ Stab ▶ controls. The arrows move the carriage by the nudge step (hold to keep going), up to the aim range either side of the plate. Guests can stab as often as they like, then tap Home. The nudge, range and arrow settings only apply to Guest aims.

Carriage positions single motor · degrees

Jog

The single motor's position counter restarts when the motor powers on. At the start of each session, put the carriage at Home (jog it or move it by hand) and press re-zero so the plate positions line up again. "Go" raises the skewer before the carriage moves.

Skewer double motor · relative degrees (left motor)

Jog

Up and Down are measured from wherever you last pressed Zero here, which resets both motors' counters to 0. The counters also restart when the motor powers on, so zero at the same spot each session (with the skewer Up is easiest). A stab goes to Down, pauses, then comes back to Up the way it went. If the two motors fight each other (buzzing or stalling), switch Right motor; a 10° jog is a safe way to check.

Cheeses changes show on the main screen right away

Saved settings

Positions, speeds and cheese names are saved in this browser and come back after a refresh. Reset returns everything to the values written at the top of cheese.html (DEFAULT_SETTINGS and CHEESES).
